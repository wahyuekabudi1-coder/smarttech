## Modern Premium Technology Theme (3-Color System)

An architectural design language crafted for global software engineering standards, utilizing a disciplined maximum 3-color palette:

1. **Ice White (`#F5F8FF`)**:
   Primary canvas background and alternating rhythm sections, delivering subtle warmth and depth compared to sterile pure white.
2. **Technology Blue (`#2563EB`)**:
   Intentional functional color for all primary CTAs, interactive links, active states, icons, and featured package borders.
3. **Deep Navy (`#172554`)**:
   Dominant authority color used for display headlines, strong labels, and selective high-impact contrast blocks (Engineering Foundation bar, enterprise footer).

### Supporting Neutrals (Strict Utility Only)
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Card surfaces, input backdrops, and modal bodies.
- **Hairline Border (`#E2E8F0`)**: Precise 1px structural contours with zero garish gradients.
- **Secondary Text (`#64748B`)**: WCAG AA compliant slate for body copy, subheadlines, and metadata.
